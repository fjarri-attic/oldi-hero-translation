# Glossary

Settled English renderings of names, places, epithets, and terms, in the order they were first established. Check here before translating a proper noun that may have appeared earlier — consistency matters more than any single "better" choice.

## The gods (coded names in the Parodos frame-narrative)

The prologue dialogue refers to the gods by relational nicknames rather than names, gradually revealing identity through context. Confirmed by later chapters where the same figures are addressed by name directly.

| Nickname (EN) | Russian | Identity | Other epithets used for them |
|---|---|---|---|
| Senior | Старший | Hades | Overlord (Владыка) |
| Middle | Средний | Poseidon | Blackmane (Черногривый), Sea Stallion (Морской Жеребец), Earth-Shaker (Колебатель Земли) |
| Junior | Младший | Zeus | — |
| Trickster | (Гермий / Лукавый) | Hermes | Hermes the Guide (Гермес-Путеводитель, elsewhere) |

"Overlord" is Senior/Hades's title of address, used by others speaking *to* him — not a separate character.

#### 16

"...Fine, we understand," the twins answered sullenly. "If we beat someone up, that was Alcides. If someone beat us up, that was Iphicles. Threw a discus farther --- that was Alcides..."

"Broke Mother's vase --- that was Alcides too," one of the brothers added, perfectly seriously, and neither Hermeias nor Chiron could tell which one had said it. Chiron decided for himself that it had been Alcides, while Hermeias decided it had been Iphicles.

"Oh, they must be looking for us at home!" the brothers suddenly remembered. "We'll catch it now... both of us. Listen, Dummy: which one of us is Alcides --- the one who gets the worse hiding, or the lighter one?"

"You'll both get it equally," Hermeias comforted them, smiling. "But not today! I'll see to that! No one will even notice you were gone... Am I a god or not?!"

"Then we're definitely getting whipped!" Alcides whispered to his brother, who at the moment cared little about Hermeias's divinity.

"Dummy, will you... well, after all this... still teach us to fight not by the rules?"

Alcides fixed the Trickster with the same questioning stare --- clearly, this mattered a great deal to both twins.

"I will, I will," Hermeias assured the brothers.

"Seems I'll have to teach you something too," Chiron drawled thoughtfully.

"Teach us what?" the brothers asked in unison. "We haven't got hooves; we can't fight the way you do."

"No need. Others will teach you to fight without my help. I'll teach you to think."

"Not by the rules?"

"Not by the rules," the centaur answered, without a trace of a smile.

#### 3

"The Moirai!"

"And I tell you --- the Eileithyiai!"

"And I say --- the Moirai!"

"Fool! As if the Moirai would sit on the threshold of some Alcmene! The very idea..."

"And sit they would, if Hera ordered it!"

"Sit, would they... The both of you are dolts! Not the Eileithyiai at all, and still less the Moirai (as if they'd listen to Hera!) --- but the sisters-Pharmakides!"

A third voice... a thirty-third... a three hundred and thirty-third voice... Thebes is abuzz, oh, how it buzzes...

"What difference does it make to you who was sitting there? The main thing is the birth was held up... Nicippe's boy in Mycenae came at seven months --- ooh, that Tantalid brood! --- and nothing happened, but with Alcmene the firstborn barely crawled out, and the second one took a whole week more..."

"Oh, sisters! Oh, just look at the fool! Show your children too, so they know! That's no woman, that's a trough of stupidity! A whole week... You've given birth yourself, fatty, you ought to know better!"

"I'm fatty? Me, fatty?! You're the fatty one!"

The women of Thebes are at each other's throats... fighting, arguing, shouting each other down. How would they know when Alcides's twin brother Iphicles was born, if the rumor about the firstborn went running the moment the babe let out his first cry, while the gossip about the second one lagged behind, a whole day late, by all accounts --- and even that, only after it was said, and heard, and believed, and checked twice over...

Thebes is abuzz, oh, how it buzzes... And the twin brothers Alcides and Iphicles sleep on, knowing nothing, caring for nothing, sucking at the breast, blowing bubbles, deaf to the foolish voices, deaf even to that one shrill voice which no one else much heard either!

"A pity!"

"What's a pity, Galinthias?"

"A pity we didn't manage to do the second sacrifice in time! Who could have known she'd bear twins... We found out too late, too late!"

"But why would we need the second one, Galinthias? The first is the hero, the Deliverer; but the second? Iphicles Amphitryad --- no great glory in that!"

"A pity... ah, a pity..."

And quiet again.

Hardly quiet though --- Thebes is abuzz, abuzz at the top of its lungs, buzzing a month, and another, and a third, buzzing half a year through...

When will it settle down?

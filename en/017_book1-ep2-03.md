#### 3

"The Moirai!"

"And I tell you---the Eileithyiai!"

"And I say---the Moirai!"

"Fool! As if the Moirai would sit on the doorstep of some Alkmene! The very idea..."

"And sit they would, if Hera ordered it!"

"Would they, now... The both of you are dolts! Not the Eileithyiai at all, and still less the Moirai (as if they'd listen to Hera!)---but the Pharmakid sisters!"

A third voice... a thirty-third... a three hundred and thirty-third voice... Thebes clamors, oh, how it clamors...

"What difference does it make to you who was sitting there? The main thing is the birth was held up... Nikippe's boy in Mycenae came at seven months---ooh, that Tantalid brood!---without a hitch, but with Alkmene the little firstborn barely squeezed his way out, and the second one came a whole week later..."

"Oh, sisters! Oh, just look at the fool! Show your children too, so they know! That's no woman, that's a whole troughful of stupidity! A whole week... You've given birth yourself, fatty, you ought to know better!"

"Who're you calling fatty? Me?! You're the fat one!"

The women of Thebes are at each other's throats... fighting, arguing, shouting each other down. How would they know when Alkeides's twin brother Iphikles was born, if the rumor about the firstborn went running the moment the babe let out his first cry, while the gossip about the second one lagged behind, held up the better part of a day---and small wonder: what with the telling, and the hearing, and the believing, and the checking...

Thebes clamors, oh, how it clamors... And the twin brothers Alkeides and Iphikles sleep on, knowing nothing, none the wiser, sucking at the breast, blowing bubbles, deaf to the foolish voices, deaf even to that one shrill voice which hardly anyone else has heard either!

"A pity!"

"What's a pity, Galinthias?"

"A pity we didn't manage to make the second sacrifice in time! Who could have known she'd bear twins... We found out too late, too late!"

"But why would we need the second one, Galinthias? The first is the hero, the Deliverer; but the second? Iphikles Amphitryad---no great glory in that!"

"A pity... ah, a pity..."

And quiet again.

Hardly quiet, though---Thebes clamors, clamors at the top of its lungs, clamors a month, and another, and a third, clamors half a year on end...

When will it settle down?

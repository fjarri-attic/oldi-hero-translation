#### 9

It was already night when Amphitryon, sitting by the fire, was approached by Buphagus, an elderly man of Pheneus, in whose house the dead man had found his last shelter before tomorrow's funeral pyre.

"Let me sit with you, Iolaus," he touched the man sitting by the fire on the shoulder, "and share your grief, if only in part. Today died your father, the great hero Iphicles, brother of god-equal Heracles --- and all of us mourn together with you."

Amphitryon slowly raised his head and looked at Buphagus.

"If you know that, you know more than I do," he answered. "Sit down."

And so they sat until dawn.

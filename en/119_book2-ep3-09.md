#### 9

It was already night when Amphitryon, sitting by the fire, was approached by Bouphagos---an elderly man of Pheneos, in whose house the dead man had found his last refuge before tomorrow's funeral pyre.

"Let me sit with you, Iolaos"---he touched the shoulder of the man sitting by the fire---"and share your grief, if only in part. Your father, the great hero Iphikles, brother of god-equal Herakles, died today---and we all mourn with you."

Amphitryon slowly raised his head and looked at Bouphagos.

"If you know that, you know more than I do," he answered. "Sit down."

And so they sat until dawn.

## Book One. Sacrifices

::: {.epigraph}
Despite all the conviction of modern man that the gods of Greece are nothing but a product of the imagination and that they never existed anywhere, all these gods stand before us---Zeus, Hera, Athena, Aphrodite, and even lesser gods, nymphs, and satyrs---clothed in flesh and blood, and so vividly that it takes considerable mental effort to free oneself of this glittering vision.
:::

::: {.epigraph-source}
---W. Aly, *RE XVI, 1376, s. v. Mythos*
